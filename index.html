Upload a photo of your build and a photo of how it should look. We’ll compare them and show exactly what to change.

  1. Photograph both. Your build as it stands, and how it should look — a finished photo, or a page from the instructions.
  2. They get lined up. The two photos are warped onto the same viewpoint and colour-matched, then compared region by region rather than as whole images, which is what makes a small missing brick findable.
  3. You get marked-up results. Each problem is pinned on your own photo. Upload an instruction page and the printed set number is read too, which unlocks the exact bricks to order.

It is automated, and it is wrong sometimes — it can miss a real mistake or flag something that is fine. If it gets one wrong, use the report button on the results; that is what improves it.
